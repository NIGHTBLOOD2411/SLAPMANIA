# M15 — ArenaVFX

> **One-liner:** Particle systems and visual effects across the arena — impact bursts, crowd confetti, ambient atmosphere, and beat-synced pulses.

## 1. Purpose

The arena is alive with particles. Impact hits produce bursts of sweat, spit, and shockwaves. The crowd throws confetti on crits. Ambient haze and spotbeam cones give the venue atmosphere. All of it is driven by gameplay events and synced to the BeatClock where appropriate.

**Status (2026-09-26):** the **ambient** half has landed with the generated arena ([ADR-0012](../Decisions/ADR-0012-arena-mobile-fakes.md)): beams, haze, crowd life, the floor pool and contact shadows. So has the arena **set** those effects sit in, which no other module owns. **Not yet:** gameplay VFX (impact shockwave, sweat and spit, confetti) and beat-synced pulses.

## 1a. Look reference

Concept frames in [`Docs/Reference/Arena/`](../Reference/Arena) (`mag_*`, `wa_*`) — the arena visual language this module has to hit.

- **Volumetric spot cones** through the crowd. Not real volumetric lighting — a cheap cone mesh with an additive alpha gradient, one per truss light. This is the effect that "sells" the venue; without it the truss rig is just point lights.
- **Ambient haze sheet** (opaque-ish soft particles or a scrolling billboard) at seating-row height, giving the beams something to catch. Kept sparse to protect the overdraw budget.
- **Crowd phone-flash sparkle** — a low-density particle system on the `Arena` layer emitting one-frame white points at random spectator seat positions, ~4–8/s. Not per-fighter, not per-beat — it is background life.
- **Sponsor-board ambience** — no VFX, but the LED apron ribbon (see M17) subtly emissive-pulses; that lives in M14/M17, not here.
- **Ring-floor pool light** — a soft additive decal on the ring canvas so the trunk casts a fake bloom halo even at Low tier.

None of this reads on the fighter — impact VFX still owns that.

## 2. Responsibilities and non-goals

**Owns**
- Impact VFX — shockwave ring, sweat/spit particles, screen-edge flash.
- Crowd VFX — confetti burst on crit, streamers on finisher, paper rain on victory.
- Ambient VFX — haze/fog particles, light beam cones, dust motes.
- Beat-synced VFX — ring-edge pulse on beat, floor glow ripple.
- Per-tier VFX quality scaling.

**Explicitly does NOT do**
- Own post-processing effects (bloom, vignette, color grading) → URP Volume.
- Own screen shake → M08/M16.
- Own the smear mesh → M08.
- Own lighting → M14.

## 3. Known constraints

- **Overdraw is the budget, not particle count.** Tile-based mobile GPUs (Mali, Adreno, Apple) are bottlenecked by overdraw, not by vertex count. The target is **≤ 3× overdraw** at peak VFX (mid-tier). This means: small particles, short lifetimes, opaque where possible, and no full-screen alpha overlays.
- **VFX Graph vs Particle System.** VFX Graph runs on compute and is not supported on GLES3. The project must support GLES3 as a fallback. Therefore **use the built-in Particle System**, not VFX Graph, for all effects. If GLES3 support is dropped in the future, migration to VFX Graph is a separate effort.
- **`FighterFX` layer (7)** — impact particles on this layer appear in snapshot photos. Ambient particles should be on the `Arena` layer (8) to avoid cluttering photos.
- **BeatClock integration** — beat-synced effects read `IBeatSource.OnBeat` for discrete pulses and `IBeatSource.CurrentBeat` (fractional) for continuous animations.
- **Device tier:**
  - Low: impact shockwave only, no ambient particles, no confetti.
  - Mid: impact shockwave + sweat, ambient haze, confetti on crit.
  - High: full set including dust motes, light beams, beat-synced floor glow.

### Deviations from §3, tracked

- **Ambient life uses mesh + shader fakes, not Particle Systems.** Phone flashes are computed in the crowd shader, and haze is noise sheets. This follows the look reference's own "cheap cone mesh" and "scrolling billboard" notes and costs no simulation. Particle Systems remain the plan for gameplay VFX.
- **No `Arena` layer (8) assignment.** The layers do not exist yet; adding them is a ProjectSettings change (R9).
- **Overdraw is unmeasured.** The beams, three haze sheets and the pool are large additive surfaces. The ≤ 3× budget needs a device capture (BASELINE) before the High-tier default is trusted.
- **The crowd shader is the biggest per-pixel cost in the arena** (3 SDF evaluations per crowd pixel). It needs a GPU capture on Mali/Adreno; the fallback is a Low-tier material with a single evaluation.

## 4. Open questions

- [ ] Should impact VFX scale with damage (larger shockwave for higher damage), or is tier-based (Normal/Crit/Finisher) sufficient?
- [ ] How many active particle systems can run simultaneously at 60 fps on mid-tier? Need a profiling pass with placeholder effects.
- [ ] Should ambient particles (haze, dust) be pre-warmed or fade in at bout start? Pre-warming avoids a visible "filling" period but costs a frame spike at load.
- [ ] Is a GPU-driven shockwave (a single expanding quad with a shader) cheaper than a particle-based one? Likely yes for the ring shape.
- [ ] Should confetti have physics (falling, bouncing) or just visual (sprite animation)? Physics costs CPU; sprite animation looks cheaper but less convincing.
- [ ] Should the beat-synced floor glow use a custom shader or a projected decal? Decals interact with URP's Render Graph and may have compatibility issues.

## 5. Dependencies

- **Hard:** `SlapMania.Core` (signals), M11 (`IBeatSource`).
- **Soft:** M05 (damage tier for VFX scaling), M08 (impact timing).
- **Unity APIs:** `ParticleSystem`, `ParticleSystemRenderer`, potentially custom shaders.
- **Must initialize after:** M00.
