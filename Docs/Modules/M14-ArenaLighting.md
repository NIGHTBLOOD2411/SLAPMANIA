# M14 — ArenaLighting

> **One-liner:** Realtime party lighting — beat-synced color chases, impact flashes, and intensity-driven mood shifts across the arena.

## 1. Purpose

The arena is a live event venue. The lighting must feel like a concert — colored spotlights sweeping the ring, strobes on beat, a flash on impact, and a slow color shift as the match intensity rises. All of it synced to the BeatClock (M11).

**Status (2026-09-26):** the bout arena's lighting has landed as part of the generated arena ([ADR-0012](../Decisions/ADR-0012-arena-mobile-fakes.md)). It covers the static look, the impact flash, device tiering and a procedural beam sweep. **Not yet:** beat-synced chases (they need M11's `IBeatSource`) and the intensity-driven mood shift.

## 1a. Look reference

Concept frames in [`Docs/Reference/Arena/`](../Reference/Arena) (`mag_*`, `wa_*`). The mood to hit:

- **Truss rig above the ring** — parallel rows of ~40 white front-of-house spots plus a smaller row of colored accent heads (red + cool blue) raking down through **volumetric haze**. Haze is doing half the work; without it the beams disappear.
- **Ring key light** — one hard warm spot pooled on the ring centre so the trunk/pedestal reads as the compositional focus; the crowd falls into shadow around it.
- **Rim + backlight on the ring apron** — red-orange rim from the far side that outlines fighters against the dark venue.
- **Crowd sparkle** — hundreds of tiny point-source phone flashes twinkling in the stands. These are ambient, not gameplay, so they belong here (or on M15 if cheaper as an unlit particle sheet).
- **Colour temperature** — warm 3000K on the ring, cool 5600K on the crowd wash. The contrast is what sells "spotlight on stage."

## 1b. What landed

All generated by `SlapMania.Editor.Arena.ArenaBuilder` (M13) into `Proto_Bout`, and run by `ArenaDirector` (`SlapMania.Presentation`, `Presentation/Arena/`):

- **Four spots plus one directional, and no more.** Four is exactly `Mobile_RPAsset`'s additional-lights-per-object limit.
  - **Key:** a spot straight down on the podium from the truss (y 8.7 m), 48°. It casts soft shadows; mobile has additional-light shadows off, so this is PC only and the blob quads (M15) carry contact on mobile.
  - **Rim left:** red, from behind-left.
  - **Rim right:** cool blue, from behind-right.
  - **Fill:** cool and low, from the camera side.
  - **Moon:** a dim directional with no shadows, set as `RenderSettings.sun`, for shape in the dark.
- **`ArenaLookConfig`** (`SlapMania.Data`, `Data/Prototype/ArenaLook.asset`, listed in the Tuning Hub) holds every lighting value. `ArenaDirector` applies it on `Awake` and again whenever the asset changes: `OnValidate` bumps a revision, and nothing is polled field by field. Ambient, fog and reflection intensity belong to the *active* scene, so they are applied once the bout scene is active (GameRoot activates it after the additive load). The builder also writes them into the saved scene.
- **Impact flash** (answers the open question below: a **light surge, not a post-process**). `ArenaDirector.ImpactFlash(strength)` multiplies the key by `1 + keyFlashBoost·k²`, and every beam cone and lamp flare's `_Intensity` by `1 + beamFlashBoost·k²` (via `MaterialPropertyBlock`). It decays on **unscaled** time so it reads during hitstop. `BoutController` calls it on `ImpactFrame`: 1.0 on a lethal slap, otherwise damage-scaled.
- **Beam sweep:** the swept beams oscillate ±`sweepDegrees` around their base aim with a `sweepPeriodMs` period.
- **Tiering:** `ArenaDirector.Tier` High/Low. Low switches off the beams, the haze and light pool, and the damage-cam camera.
- **Reflections:** one probe baked at build time, pinned as a custom cubemap and as the scene's default reflection.
- **Where the look comes from:** the reference's warm key vs cool crowd contrast is carried by the warm key, the cool fill and moon, and the crowd shader's height wash (red near the ring, violet up high; M15). The rims give the red/blue outline.

## 2. Responsibilities and non-goals

**Owns**
- Arena spotlights and their color/intensity animation.
- Beat-synced light chases (patterns that follow the BPM).
- Impact flash — a full-arena burst on the impact frame.
- Intensity-driven ambient color (cool blues at low intensity, hot reds at high).
- Rim lights on fighters (appear only in snapshots via the `SnapshotOnly` layer).

**Explicitly does NOT do**
- Own the main directional light → scene setup.
- Own post-processing bloom → URP Volume on the main camera.
- Own VFX particles → M15.
- Own the beat clock → M11.

## 3. Known constraints

- **URP 17, Render Graph ON.** Any custom light passes must use `RecordRenderGraph`, not the legacy `Execute` path.
- **Mobile GPU budget:** arena lights are a significant contributor to the 3.0 ms transparents/particles budget in [BASELINE.md](../Perf/BASELINE.md). Realtime shadows from spotlights are likely too expensive — baked shadow maps or no shadows.
- **Point and spot light limits:** URP's Forward+ path handles many lights efficiently on desktop but may exceed the per-object light limit on mobile GLES3. Test with the actual arena geometry.
- **BeatClock integration:** light animations should be driven by `IBeatSource.OnBeat` and `IBeatSource.CurrentBeat`, not by `Time.time`. The beat is the metronome; lights that drift from the music feel broken.
- **Snapshot interaction:** rim lights on the `SnapshotOnly` layer (10) appear only in photos, not in the live view. This gives photos a studio-lit quality without the performance cost of extra lights every frame.
- **Device tier:** Low tier should disable animated spotlights and use a single ambient color shift. Mid tier gets 4 spotlights. High tier gets 8+ with sweep animations.

### Deviations from §3, tracked

- **Beam sweep runs on `Time.time`, not `IBeatSource`.** M11 does not exist yet. Move the sweep phase onto `CurrentBeat` when it lands.
- **Rims are realtime in the live view, not `SnapshotOnly`-layer lights.** The project has no `Arena` / `FighterFX` / `SnapshotOnly` layers yet (adding layers is a ProjectSettings change, R9). Revisit with M09.
- **No Mid tier yet.** High is the full set; Low drops the fakes. The lights themselves are not tiered.

## 4. Open questions

- [ ] How many realtime lights can the arena support at 60 fps on mid-tier? Need a profiling pass. *Landed at 4 spots plus 1 directional (the mobile per-object limit); unmeasured on device.*
- [x] Should the light chase pattern be authored as an animation curve per light, or as a procedural sweep driven by beat position? **Procedural sweep** (per-beam phase offset); beat-driven once M11 exists.
- [x] Should the impact flash be a point light burst or a screen-space post-process (vignette flash)? **Neither.** The existing key light and the additive fakes surge, so the scene is lit honestly with no extra light.
- [ ] Should the rim lights for snapshots be realtime or baked into the fighter material as a fresnel effect?
- [ ] How does the color temperature shift with intensity? A curve from 6500K (neutral) to 3500K (warm) would match the "heating up" metaphor.
- [ ] Should lights react to the specific zone hit (red flash on Perfect, blue on Miss), or only to the damage magnitude?

## 5. Dependencies

- **Hard:** `SlapMania.Core` (signals), M11 (`IBeatSource`).
- **Soft:** M08 (impact timing for flash).
- **Unity APIs:** `Light`, `RenderSettings` (ambient trilight, fog, custom reflection), `MaterialPropertyBlock`, `ReflectionProbe` (baked in the editor by `Lightmapping.BakeReflectionProbe`).
- **Assets:** `ArenaLookConfig` (Data), the `ArenaPost` Volume profile (colour grade; ACES, Bloom, Colour Adjustments, Lift/Gamma/Gain, Vignette, Split Toning).
- **Must initialize after:** M00, M11.
